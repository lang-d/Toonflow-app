# Story Bible Generation

Use this when the user has chosen a direction and needs stable story foundations.

Include:
- title and logline
- genre, tone, target audience, format
- world and setting rules
- protagonist, antagonist, supporting characters
- character desires, wounds, contradictions, and relationships
- central conflict
- theme
- ending direction
- production notes for visual adaptation

Keep the bible consistent and avoid overexplaining details that can be discovered through drama.
