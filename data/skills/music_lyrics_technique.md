# Music Lyrics Technique

Draft lyrics for one confirmed music edition.

Define the speaker, addressee, tense, knowledge boundary, and emotional contradiction before writing. Do not reveal plot facts the song's perspective should not know.

Use section labels only when useful. Keep lines singable, with controlled syllable density and natural stress in the target language. Build repetition around one hook; repeated lines should gain context or emotional weight rather than fill space.

Prefer concrete sensory or relational images over abstract slogans. Avoid screenplay narration, character resumes, exposition, forced rhyme, mixed point of view, and a new metaphor in every line.

AI output is always a draft. Preserve any supplied user lines, identify them as constraints, and never mark the result confirmed.
