# Music Review Rules

Review only planning and prompt quality. Do not evaluate generated audio.

Music bible review:
- fits project type, story theme, and character relationships
- defines motifs, palette, avoid list, and silence strategy
- avoids generic style-word piles
- does not treat director planning as the music authority

Music plan and cue sheet review:
- cues are divided by musical semantics, not mechanically per storyboard
- opening theme, ending theme, insert song, BGM, stinger, ambient, source music, and silence have clear responsibilities
- plan is consistent with the music bible
- episode mode supports rolling serialized production

Cue prompt review:
- follows the target model prompt profile
- includes duration, emotional arc, instrumentation, structure, vocal/lyrics policy, and avoid/negative prompt
- is not too long or too scattered
- does not include full story material or visual-only instructions

Generation result review is out of scope:
- do not judge whether audio sounds good
- do not listen to or analyze generated audio
- only engineering checks may verify file existence, asset ownership, state, and errorReason
