# Story Outline Generation

Use this for story structure.

Support three levels:
- whole outline: acts, turns, midpoint, climax, ending
- episode outline: hook, event, conflict, reversal, ending hook
- scene cards: location, characters, action, emotional beat, visual potential

For short drama, emphasize early hook, conflict density, reversals, and ending hooks.
Do not generate full dialogue unless the user asks for script writing.
