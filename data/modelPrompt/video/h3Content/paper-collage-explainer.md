# H3 single-shot expression skill: paper-collage explainer

## Scope and fact inheritance

Use this layer only where the formal `videoStyle` or current storyboard facts already establish editorial paper collage, cut-paper, halftone, photographic cut-out, tactile assembly, or a collage explainer treatment. It adapts one existing shot; it does not extract a new topic, invent a visual metaphor, write narration, add cards/diagrams, generate stills, plan a full explainer, or assemble clips.

Preserve the supplied subject, factual relationships, action result, camera, dialogue, visible text, and sound. The formal `videoStyle` is stronger than this type layer: do not convert an established realistic, 3D, illustrated, or other medium into collage merely because the user selected a type.

## Collage visual grammar

When supported, express the existing scene through deliberate layered cut-paper relationships: bold paper fields, controlled halftone texture, photographic cut-outs, selective cardstock accents, warm keylines, soft paper shadows, and clear foreground/midground/background hierarchy. Use these as physical evidence of an already approved collage treatment, never as generic decorative noise. Keep the factual focal object readable in its original spatial role.

Build changes through tactile assembly: a cut-out slides, pops, presses, pivots, layers over another plane, or settles into the confirmed state. Make one stated transformation readable at a time. Do not add letters, numerals, logos, subtitles, labels, charts, icons, a presenter, or a new explanatory metaphor when the facts do not contain them.

## Timeline compilation

Write a concrete progression: existing paper-field or subject state -> fact-supported assembly/movement/change -> confirmed result with a brief tactile settle. Prefer the supplied camera and an existing compositional shift over generic slow zoom, drifting layers, or arbitrary transitions. Describe paper clicks, pops, slides, presses, soft taps, or rustles only when tied to a visible supported action or already supplied as diegetic sound.

Retain supplied narration/dialogue exactly and do not create voice-over. `overall_soundscape` may include factual ambience and supported collage SFX, but never BGM, score, or automatic music.

## Missing-fact policy and boundaries

Without evidence of collage treatment, paper movement, a visual metaphor, visible text, or paper SFX, omit it. Do not manufacture explanation steps, abstract symbols, captions, cards, diagrams, narration, music, or a second scene.

Do not override the formal `videoStyle`, chronology and duration, keyframe/reference roles, media labels, dialogue, visible text, diegetic sound, or the no-BGM rule.

## Final check

The final prompt must use supported collage material and assembly as the mechanism of the existing shot's change; it must not be a generic “paper collage” label added to unrelated content.
