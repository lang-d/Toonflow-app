# H3 single-shot expression skill: brand promotion

## Scope and fact inheritance

Use this layer to present an already evidenced brand, product, service, interface, shop, or project fact clearly within one supplied shot. It is not an intake, source-research, brand-truth-sheet, provenance, campaign-planning, asset-generation, voice-over, music, editing, or delivery workflow. Treat only user-provided or explicitly defined references and storyboard facts as verifiable asset truth. Never infer a logo, brand colour, product capability, audience, promise, comparison, compliance claim, CTA, or source authority from an asset name or general knowledge.

The formal `videoStyle` remains authoritative. Preserve any supplied mark, product appearance, UI, copy, colour system, and visual identity exactly; do not imitate an unprovided real brand or replace the approved visual language with a generic corporate style.

## Promotional single-shot grammar

Choose one evidenced communication focus: a confirmed product state, UI interaction, use case, before/after result, brand mark, exact copy, or CTA. Arrange the existing opening state, action, and result so this one focus is readable. Camera emphasis may clarify the focus through a supplied or necessary follow movement, but must not create a new demo, montage, screen recording, feature tour, customer reaction, or multi-panel layout.

For an existing interface, preserve the confirmed screen, visible UI order, interaction result, and text verbatim. Do not invent buttons, metrics, charts, workflows, errors, notifications, or device behaviour. For physical products, describe visible structure/material/action only when shown by the facts. Keep any supplied logo or copy stable and legible at the stated time; no logo, copy, or CTA means no logo, copy, or CTA instruction.

## Timeline compilation

Write a concise fact-led progression: establish the supplied identity or focal state -> show the confirmed interaction/reveal/change -> hold the confirmed result long enough to read. Do not spend time on an empty establishing shot or introduce unrelated visual spectacle. If the supplied duration includes more than one factual state, use an existing action, visual match, or camera continuity to connect them; do not add a campaign-style transition merely because it is promotional.

Keep dialogue, visible text, and diegetic sound in their supplied chronology. Native H3 audio may carry only the existing sound facts. Never add brand music, slogan voice-over, testimonial, applause, or marketing narration.

## Missing-fact policy and boundaries

Omit unsupported claims, copy, features, user scenarios, branding, UI, camera moves, and sound. Do not turn a neutral shot into an advertisement by fabricating benefits. Do not use a reference image as proof of a role it was not assigned.

Do not override the formal `videoStyle`, storyboard facts or duration, keyframe/reference roles, `<Picture N>` / `<Video N>` / `<Audio N>` labels, dialogue, visible text, diegetic sound, or the no-BGM rule.

## Final check

The final prompt must identify one verifiable promotional focus and make only that existing fact easier to read; it must contain no invented brand promise, logo, claim, CTA, UI, or music.
